# GateTimeline

The 30-day strip shows a person's position in the programme, with the three gates marked.

Thirty cells in phases of 15, 6 and 9 days. `ds-day--done` for completed days, `ds-day--today` for the current day, `ds-day--gate` for Days 15, 21 and 30 (add `ds-day--done` when cleared, which turns them `good`). Under 760px wrap to 15 columns. The consumer provides the current day and gate results.
