# AssignmentPreview

The assignment preview shows who a new joinee will be given before HR adds them: Reporting Boss, Mentor and HR partner.

Three `out` tiles in a row (one column on phones), each with an eyebrow naming the rule ("Reporting Boss · same dept", "Mentor · other dept", "HR partner · region"), the person's name in `label` weight, and a `caption` line with department, region, experience and load. A pill on the right of the header states whether the joinee fits the persona. Recompute on every change to department, region, experience or a manual override. The consumer provides the three people, their loads and the persona result.
