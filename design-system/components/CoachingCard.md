# CoachingCard

The coaching card is where a mentor, manager or HR partner records the outcome of a one-day coaching session.

Shows trainee, gate and score, module-level bars (`crit` below 70%, `warn` 70–79%, `good` 80%+), the other coaches' progress, a notes field (minimum 30 characters, kept in the audit trail), module checkboxes pre-ticked below 70%, and two actions. The decision rule is shown to coaches: any single "repeat" assigns the ticked modules. The consumer provides the escalation data and handlers.
