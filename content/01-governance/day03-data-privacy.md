# Day 3 — Data privacy and information security
## DPDP Act 2023 essentials for RMs
- Use personal data only for the **purpose** it was collected for, with **consent** or a legitimate use.
- Collect the **minimum** needed; keep it only as long as needed.
- Individuals have rights to access, correct and erase; breaches must be reported through the bank's incident process.

## Daily do's and don'ts
| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Use bank-approved channels and devices | Forward client data to personal email or WhatsApp |
| Lock your screen; clear desk | Photograph documents on your phone |
| Use the secure upload link for client documents | Share CIF lists with vendors without an approved contract |
| Report phishing and lost devices immediately | Paste client data into public AI tools |

The Academy assistant automatically masks PAN, Aadhaar and account numbers — but you should never paste them in the first place.
