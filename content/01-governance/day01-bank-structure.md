# Day 1 — The bank, the corporate bank and how it makes money
**Pillar:** Governance · **Time:** 60 min · **Outcome:** explain where an RM sits and what drives value.

## How a large private bank is organised
- **Business units:** Corporate & Commercial Banking (large, mid, emerging corporates), Retail, Treasury, Transaction Banking.
- **Control functions (independent of business):** Credit Risk, Compliance, Internal Audit, Operations, Legal, Finance.
- **Three lines of defence:** (1) business owns risk — that is you, the RM; (2) risk & compliance set and monitor limits; (3) internal audit gives independent assurance.

## How the corporate bank earns
| Source | Examples | Capital intensive? |
|---|---|---|
| Net interest income (NII) | Cash credit, WCDL, term loans | Yes |
| Float / liability income | Current accounts, CMS, payroll, escrow | No |
| Fee income | LC, BG, processing fees, syndication | Low |
| Treasury income | FX conversion, hedges | Low |

**Key idea:** the best relationships combine credit with fee and float products — this lifts return on capital (you will calculate this on Day 19).
