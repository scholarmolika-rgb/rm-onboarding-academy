# Day 3 — AML red flags: case vignettes
For each case: identify the red flag, what you do, and whom you inform. Discuss with your mentor.

## Case 1 — Structured cash
A trading firm deposits ₹9.5 lakh in cash on four consecutive days. **Flag:** structuring below thresholds. **Action:** do not advise the client on thresholds; escalate for STR review.

## Case 2 — Circular trading
Large receipts from three entities that share a registered address with the client, immediately paid out to a fourth. **Flag:** round-tripping. **Action:** escalate; review group structure and beneficial ownership.

## Case 3 — Unexplained third-party payments
Export proceeds arrive from a country unrelated to the stated buyer. **Flag:** trade-based money laundering / FEMA concern. **Action:** seek documents; route to trade compliance.

## Case 4 — Reluctant beneficial owner
Promoter refuses to disclose who holds 30% through an offshore trust. **Action:** onboarding cannot proceed without BO identification.

## Case 5 — Sudden turnover spike
Dormant current account shows turnover jumping 20× with no business rationale. **Action:** enquire, record, escalate.

**Golden rule:** never tip off the client that a report is being considered.
