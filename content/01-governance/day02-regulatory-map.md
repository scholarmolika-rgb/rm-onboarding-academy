# Day 2 — The regulatory map for a corporate RM
Always check the **latest RBI Master Directions** and the bank's internal policies; this page is an orientation, not the rulebook.

## Who regulates what
| Regulator / law | Why it matters to an RM |
|---|---|
| **RBI** (Banking Regulation Act) | Lending norms, KYC Master Direction, IRACP (asset classification), interest-rate directions, fair practices code |
| **PMLA 2002 & PML Rules** | KYC, beneficial ownership, CTR/STR reporting to FIU-IND |
| **FEMA 1999** | Forex transactions, ECBs, export/import payments, hedging eligibility |
| **SEBI** | Listed-client disclosures, insider information in the bank's possession |
| **DPDP Act 2023** | Lawful, purpose-limited processing of personal data |
| **Companies Act / IBC** | Charges, directors, insolvency process |

## Rules you will use weekly
- **KYC periodic updation:** high-risk every 2 years, medium every 8, low every 10.
- **Beneficial owner (company):** natural person with more than 10% ownership or control.
- **CTR:** cash transactions above ₹10 lakh (or connected series in a month); **STR:** within 7 working days of the suspicion conclusion.
- **Asset classification:** SMA-0 (1–30 days overdue), SMA-1 (31–60), SMA-2 (61–90), NPA (> 90).
