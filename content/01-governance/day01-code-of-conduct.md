# Day 1 — Code of conduct, conflicts and whistleblowing
## Principles
Act honestly, in the client's legitimate interest and the bank's, within the law. When in doubt: **pause, ask, record.**

## Conflicts of interest
- Declare any personal or family relationship with a client, vendor or colleague in a reporting line.
- Never approve, recommend or influence a decision where you have a personal interest.
- Outside employment, directorships and investments in client companies need prior approval.

## Gifts and hospitality
- Token gifts within the policy limit: accept and **declare in the gift register**.
- Anything above the limit, cash or cash-equivalents, or **anything offered while a decision is pending**: decline politely and report.

## Whistleblowing
Concerns about fraud, mis-selling or misconduct can be raised through the vigil mechanism, anonymously if needed. Retaliation against a whistleblower is itself misconduct.

## Consequence management
Breaches are handled through the staff accountability framework and may lead to disciplinary action — and regulators can hold individuals accountable.
