# Days 22–29 — Shadowing playbook
You observe and then co-lead live client interactions with your mentor. **You do not hold independent authority** — all advice, pricing and documentation is owned by the mentor.

## Daily routine
1. Morning brief with mentor: clients, objectives, your role (observe / co-lead).
2. Interactions: calls, visits, credit discussions, KYC reviews, complaint handling.
3. Log each interaction in the Academy (masked client reference only — never names, PAN or account numbers).
4. Evening reflection: what went well, what you would do differently.

## Competency rubric (mentor rates 1–5)
| Competency | 5 looks like |
|---|---|
| Needs discovery | Uncovers cash-cycle and growth needs unprompted |
| Product fit | Proposes a coherent multi-product structure |
| Pricing & P&L | Explains pricing on relationship value; within authority |
| Compliance & conduct | Spots KYC/AML/data issues and escalates correctly |
| Communication | Clear, confident, client-centric |
| Ownership | Follows up, updates CRM same day |

**Progression:** Days 22–24 observe · 25–27 co-lead · 28–29 lead with mentor present. Target average ≥ 3.5 before Day 30.
