# Day 10 — Treasury, FX hedging and liability products
- **FX conversion:** spot, forward contracts. Importers with USD payables buy USD forward; exporters with receivables sell forward.
- **Derivatives:** options and swaps require suitability and user-classification checks — route through treasury sales.
- **Liability products:** current accounts, fixed deposits, sweep-in FDs. Current-account balances are valued at the FTP credit rate (Day 19).
