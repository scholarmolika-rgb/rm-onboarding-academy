# Day 6 — Working capital finance
## Products
- **Cash Credit (CC):** running limit against stock and receivables; interest on daily utilised balance.
- **Overdraft (OD):** against FDs, property or cash flows.
- **Working Capital Demand Loan (WCDL):** short tenor tranche carved out of the fund-based limit, usually cheaper.

## Drawing power (DP)
`DP = (Eligible stock + Eligible receivables − Trade creditors) × (1 − margin)`

**Example:** stock ₹100 Cr, receivables ₹60 Cr, creditors ₹40 Cr, margin 25% → (100 + 60 − 40) × 0.75 = **₹90 Cr**. Utilisation is capped at the lower of limit and DP.

## Assessing the limit
Understand the operating cycle; banks use turnover or cash-budget methods for assessing working capital. Watch for DP inflation (old or obsolete stock, ageing receivables).
