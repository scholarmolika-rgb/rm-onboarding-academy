# Day 7 — Term loans and project finance basics
- **Purpose:** capex, expansion, acquisitions. Tenor typically 3–10 years with moratorium.
- **DSCR** = (PAT + depreciation + interest on term loan) ÷ (interest + principal due). Banks usually look for comfortable coverage above 1.2–1.3x; 1.0x means no cushion.
- **Security:** hypothecation/mortgage of assets; register charges (ROC) and security interest (CERSAI within 30 days).
- **Covenants:** leverage, DSCR, promoter holding, restrictions on dividends — formalised in the sanction letter and facility agreement.
