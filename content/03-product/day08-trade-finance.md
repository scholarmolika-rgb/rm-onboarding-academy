# Day 8 — Trade finance
| Product | Use |
|---|---|
| Import LC | Bank's undertaking to pay the seller on compliant documents |
| Bank guarantee (performance/financial) | Contingent undertaking on client's obligation |
| Bill discounting (LC-backed or not) | Early cash against receivables |
| Packing credit / PCFC | Pre-shipment export finance (PCFC in foreign currency) |
| Post-shipment credit | Finance after shipment until export proceeds arrive |

Non-fund products earn **fees** and consume less capital than loans — central to relationship profitability. All trade must comply with FEMA and sanctions screening.
