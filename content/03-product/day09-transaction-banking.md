# Day 9 — Transaction banking
- **Cash management (CMS):** collections from many locations, payables automation, liquidity sweeps, host-to-host integration.
- **Payroll accounts:** salary for employees — float for the bank, retail cross-sell opportunity.
- **Supply chain finance:** dealer finance (anchor's distributors) and vendor finance (anchor's suppliers).
- **Escrow and virtual accounts** for reconciliation.

Why it matters: CMS and payroll build **low-cost current-account float** and make the relationship sticky — harder for competitors to take over.
