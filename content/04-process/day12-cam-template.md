# Day 12 — Credit Appraisal Memo (CAM) template
Use this structure for your practice CAM on Apex Auto Components.
1. **Proposal summary** — facilities, amounts, tenor, pricing, security.
2. **Borrower profile** — promoters, group, business model, industry outlook.
3. **Financial analysis** — 3 years: revenue, EBITDA margin, leverage, current ratio, DSCR, cash conversion cycle.
4. **Assessment of limits** — working capital method, DP computation, capex funding.
5. **Risk analysis** — business, financial, management, industry; mitigants.
6. **Security and covenants.**
7. **Pricing and RAROC** — attach calculator output.
8. **Compliance** — KYC status, exposure norms, deviations with approvals.
9. **Recommendation.**
