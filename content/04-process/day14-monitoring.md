# Day 14 — Monitoring, EWS and asset classification
## Early warning signals
Delayed stock statements, frequent cheque returns, persistent over-utilisation, falling credits in the account, auditor resignation, adverse news, promoter pledge increases.

## Classification
| Overdue | Status |
|---|---|
| 1–30 days | SMA-0 |
| 31–60 days | SMA-1 |
| 61–90 days | SMA-2 |
| > 90 days | NPA |
A CC/OD becomes NPA if **out of order continuously for 90 days**.

## Renewals
Start 60 days before due date; an overdue renewal is itself an EWS and an audit finding.
