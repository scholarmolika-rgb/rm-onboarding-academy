# Day 18 — Fees and pricing deviations
- **Fees:** processing (e.g., 1% of ₹20 Cr = ₹20 lakh), LC/BG commission on average utilisation, CMS charges, FX margins.
- **Deviations:** any price below card rate needs approval from the delegated authority, with a written rationale (relationship RAROC, competitive quote, cross-sell committed). Deviations are audited — track promised cross-sell and report if it does not materialise.
