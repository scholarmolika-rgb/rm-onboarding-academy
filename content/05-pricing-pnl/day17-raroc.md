# Day 17 — Risk-based pricing and RAROC
- **Expected loss (EL) = PD × LGD × EAD.** Example: 2% × 45% × ₹50 Cr = ₹45 lakh.
- **Capital** = RWA × capital ratio. RWA = exposure × risk weight (e.g., AAA-rated corporate 20% under the standardised approach). Example: ₹100 Cr × 20% × 11.5% = ₹2.3 Cr.
- **RAROC** = (Income − Operating cost − EL − Tax) ÷ Capital. Compare with the bank's hurdle rate.

If RAROC is below hurdle: reprice, restructure security, or add fee and float business — or seek a documented deviation.
