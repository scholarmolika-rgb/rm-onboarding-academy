# Day 16 — How loans are priced
`Customer rate = Benchmark (EBLR / MCLR / T-bill) + Spread`

The **spread** covers: cost of funds above benchmark, operating cost, expected credit loss, cost of capital, and profit.
- **External benchmark (EBLR):** resets at least once every three months.
- **Funds Transfer Pricing (FTP):** treasury charges you for funds and credits you for deposits. Your **NII = (yield − FTP) × balance**.

**Example:** ₹10 Cr at 9.50%, FTP 7.00% → NII ₹25 lakh per year.
