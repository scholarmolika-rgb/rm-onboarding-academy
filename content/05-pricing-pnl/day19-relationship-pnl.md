# Day 19 — Relationship P&L
```
  Net interest income (loans)       (yield − FTP) × average balance
+ Liability income (float)          (FTP credit − rate paid) × average CA/FD balance
+ Fee income                        processing, LC, BG, CMS, FX
= Total income
− Operating cost                    (cost-to-income × income, or activity-based)
− Expected loss                     PD × LGD × EAD
= Profit before tax
− Tax                               (~25.17%)
= Profit after tax
÷ Allocated capital                 RWA × capital ratio
= Relationship RoE / RAROC
```
Use the **RelationshipPnL** sheet: change one lever at a time and see which moves RoE most.
