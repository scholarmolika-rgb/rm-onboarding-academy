# Day 20 — Portfolio P&L and wallet share (simulation)
- **Wallet share** = your bank's revenue from the client ÷ client's total banking revenue pool.
- Map each of your 5 practice clients: products held vs products needed; estimate uncaptured wallet.
- Build a 90-day cross-sell plan: top 3 opportunities, owner (RM or product partner), expected income, risk.
Present to your reporting manager — this becomes your first portfolio plan after certification.
