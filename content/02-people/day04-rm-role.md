# Day 4 — The Relationship Manager role
## What you own
- **Revenue:** NII, fees, float across your portfolio.
- **Risk:** portfolio quality, early warning signals, timely renewals, KYC currency.
- **Client experience:** turnaround times, complaint closure, NPS.

## Typical KRAs (confirm with your manager)
| KRA | Weight | Example metric |
|---|---|---|
| Portfolio growth | 30% | Net new limits and balances |
| Revenue & RAROC | 25% | Relationship income vs plan; RAROC ≥ hurdle |
| Asset quality | 20% | No slippage; zero overdue renewals |
| Compliance & hygiene | 15% | KYC current; zero audit findings |
| Client experience | 10% | Complaints closed within TAT |

## A day in the life
Morning: EWS dashboard and overdue list → client calls/visits → afternoon: credit notes, internal coordination → CRM updates before close.
