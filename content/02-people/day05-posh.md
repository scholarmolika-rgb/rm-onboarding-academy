# Day 5 — Respectful workplace (POSH, diversity)
- The Sexual Harassment of Women at Workplace Act 2013 (POSH) requires an **Internal Committee**; complaints can be raised with it confidentially.
- Harassment includes unwelcome physical, verbal or non-verbal conduct, including over messaging apps and at client events.
- Bystanders are encouraged to support and report.
- Inclusive behaviour with clients and colleagues is part of your conduct rating.
