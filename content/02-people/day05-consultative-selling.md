# Day 5 — Consultative conversations (simulation)
## Discovery framework
1. **Business:** what they make, sell, to whom; seasonality.
2. **Cash cycle:** debtor days, inventory days, creditor days.
3. **Growth:** capex, new markets, acquisitions.
4. **Pain:** delays, costs, forex exposure, collections.
5. **Decision makers:** CFO, promoter, treasury head.

## Objection handling — the bank next door offers 50 bps less
Acknowledge → clarify what is being compared → reframe on total relationship value (TAT, CMS, forex, advisory) → offer a structured proposal → never commit pricing outside your authority.

## Role-play (with mentor)
Scenario: CFO of a ₹400 Cr auto-component maker unhappy with current bank's slow LC processing. Run a 15-minute discovery; mentor scores you on the shadowing rubric.
