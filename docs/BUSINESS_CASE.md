# Business case — for the Head of Corporate Banking

> Planning reference only. It is not a screen in the app; it explains the data points and people the programme needs.

## The problem
Private banks lose a large share of newly hired Relationship Managers in their first year. Each exit costs the bank three times:
1. **Hiring cost** — recruiter fees, interview time, notice-period gap, re-onboarding.
2. **Productivity cost** — 4–6 months before a replacement RM covers their cost.
3. **Client cost** — relationships that follow the departing RM, or leave during a poor hand-over; plus conduct and data-leakage risk when an under-trained RM handles clients or leaves with client knowledge.

Unstructured onboarding makes this worse: new RMs meet clients before they understand KYC, pricing authority or the bank's P&L, make early mistakes, lose confidence and leave.

## What the Academy changes
- No client contact until Gate 1 (80%) and Gate 2 (75%) are cleared or three senior people formally pass the trainee.
- Every struggling trainee gets three one-day coaching sessions within 3 working days — the moment they are most at risk of disengaging.
- HR sees an attrition-risk score for all 1,000 joiners daily (test gaps + coaching events + inactivity + pulse surveys) and can act before a resignation.
- Every score, coaching note and sign-off is in an audit trail — evidence for internal audit and RBI inspection that the bank trains customer-facing staff.

## Model (illustrative — replace with the bank's numbers; the prototype's calculator does this live)

| Assumption | Value |
|---|---|
| New RMs per year | 1,000 |
| Average CTC | ₹12 lakh |
| Year-1 attrition today → with Academy | 30% → 25% |
| Replacement cost per exit | 75% of CTC = ₹9 lakh |
| Revenue per RM portfolio | ₹3 Cr/yr |
| Client revenue lost per exit | 5% = ₹15 lakh |
| Platform build + run | ₹1.2 Cr/yr |
| Coaching time (mentor/manager/HR days) | ₹0.6 Cr/yr |

| Result | Value |
|---|---|
| Exits avoided | 50 of 300 |
| Cost per exit | ₹24 lakh |
| Gross benefit | ₹12.0 Cr |
| Net annual benefit | **₹10.2 Cr** (6.7× programme cost) |
| Replacement hiring reduced | **17%** (project goal: 10%) |

Sensitivity: at half the attrition improvement (30% → 27.5%), net benefit is still about ₹4.2 Cr, but replacement hiring falls only 8%, short of the 10% goal. The goal needs attrition to drop by at least 3 percentage points (30% → 27%).

## KPIs to report monthly
Gate pass rate (first attempt), coaching SLA adherence, time-to-certification, 90-day and 12-month retention by cohort vs pre-Academy cohorts, early-tenure audit findings, first-year revenue per RM, pulse score.
